Section 01
Vision & Strategic Positioning
What FounderPort is, why it exists, and why MPB is uniquely positioned to win this market.

The Problem

Every year, thousands of Indian startups fail not because their ideas are bad — but because they can't access the right capital, at the right stage, from the right investors. The advisory ecosystem is fragmented, expensive, and inaccessible to founders outside metro networks.

Incubators manage 20–100 startups with spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. Investors receive unvetted, misaligned decks daily. Referral partners have no platform to operate through. The entire capital access chain is broken.

The Solution

FounderPort is a multi-sided AI-powered platform where startups get investor-ready, incubators manage their cohorts, investors discover mandate-matched deals, and referral partners earn by connecting the ecosystem.

Every interaction is powered by AI — from auto-generating IRR reports and pitch decks to scraping the web for matching investors. MPB's existing mandates, investor relationships, and 520+ portfolio companies give FounderPort an unfair advantage from Day 1.

Core Thesis
FounderPort does not compete with StartupLanes (community-led), Startups.com (US education-first), or Accubate (incubator ops SaaS). It is the only platform that combines AI-driven readiness assessment + live investor mandate matching + incubator cohort management in a single India-first product. The network effect compounds: more startups attract more investors, more investors attract more startups, more incubators bring batches of startups simultaneously.

Section 10
Matching Engine Architecture
The algorithmic core that connects startups to the right investors and grants.
Investor ↔ Startup Matching
Input variables (startup side):
Sector · Sub-sector · Stage · Revenue · Geography · Business model · Funding ask · Equity offered · Traction signals · IRR score

Input variables (investor side):
Sector preference · Stage focus · Ticket size range · Geography · Revenue threshold · Exclusions · Portfolio overlap

Scoring: Weighted cosine similarity across all dimensions. Probability score 0–100%. Threshold configurable by MPB admin. Top N matches surfaced to startup. All matches surfaced to investor weekly digest.
Grant ↔ Startup Matching
Matching criteria:
Sector eligibility · Stage eligibility · Entity type (Pvt Ltd / LLP etc.) · DPIIT recognition status · Location (state-specific grants) · Revenue threshold · Age of company · Founder category (woman-led, SC/ST etc.)

Output: Ranked grant list with eligibility %, amount, deadline, and missing criteria (what the startup needs to become eligible).

Daily agent: Checks for new grants and deadline changes. Alerts startups with saved grant shortlists.
EOD AI Agent — Incremental Investor Discovery
Every evening, an AI agent runs a web search across news sources, LinkedIn, and social media for: new fund raises announced (new LP-funded VCs), investors who tweeted/posted about a sector that matches pending startups, new angel network memberships announced, sector-specific investment news. Any new investors discovered are added to DB-01 with source tagging. Relevant startups receive a notification: "3 new potential investors found for you today."
Section 11
n8n Integration
Connecting FounderPort to automated outreach, follow-up workflows, and CRM actions.
Outbound Email Campaigns
Startup selects investor list → clicks "Launch Campaign" → FounderPort pushes to n8n → n8n sends personalised emails with one-pager attached. Personalisation: investor name, firm, why this startup matches their mandate. Open rates, click-throughs, replies tracked and surfaced in FounderPort dashboard.
Follow-Up Sequences
Automated follow-up if no response in 5 days. Second follow-up at 10 days. "Checking in" at 15 days. Startup can customise timing and message. Responses pause the sequence. MPB team can review and intervene manually at any point.
Consent Outreach (Scraped Startups)
When investor expresses interest in a scraped startup, n8n triggers an automated email to the startup founder: "An investor matching your profile has expressed interest. Join FounderPort to connect." On registration + consent, investor introduction is made.
Metrics Dashboard Upload
n8n exports campaign metrics as JSON. Uploaded to FounderPort (auto or manual). Dashboard shows: emails sent, open rate, response rate, meetings booked, by campaign and by startup. MPB internal dashboard aggregates across all active campaigns.

Claude API (Anthropic) for document generation — IRR, one-pager, pitch deck. Gemini API for web scraping enrichment and real-time search. Configurable — swap models per use case.
Database
PostgreSQL (main). Vector DB (Pinecone or pgvector) for semantic matching. Separate schemas per tenant for incubator isolation. Cloud: AWS / GCP (existing MPB preference).
Scraping & Agents
Playwright + BeautifulSoup for structured scraping. Apify for complex sites. AI agents (LangChain or custom) for EOD investor discovery. Cron jobs for scheduled refreshes.
Integrations
n8n (self-hosted) for outreach workflows. Razorpay for credit/subscription payments. SendGrid for transactional email. WhatsApp Business API for investor alerts (Phase 2).
DPDP Act 2023 Compliance — Critical
All scraped startup data must carry a consent flag. Before any startup's data is shared with an investor, explicit digital consent must be captured and stored with timestamp. Auto-onboarding flow must include a clear consent step. Data retention policies must be defined. Consider appointing a Data Privacy Officer (even a part-time one) before public launch.
